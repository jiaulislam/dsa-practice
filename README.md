## Topic order + sample weekly focus (first 12 weeks example)

- Week 1–2: Arrays & Strings (sliding window, two-pointers).
- Week 3: Linked Lists (reverse, cycles).
- Week 4: Stacks/Queues/Monotonic stacks.
- Week 5: Hash maps & sets patterns.
- Week 6: Sorting & binary search templates.
- Week 7–8: Trees (traversals, LCA, BST ops).
- Week 9: Recursion & Backtracking (subsets, permutations).
- Week 10: Heaps & priority queues.
- Week 11: Graphs 101 (BFS/DFS, components).
- Week 12: Review + mixed contest / timed problems.

Each week: two concept days, three problem days, one mixed/timed day, one rest day.
